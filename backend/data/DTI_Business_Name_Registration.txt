DTI Business Name Registration Guidelines
Source: Department of Trade and Industry — Business Name Registration System (BNRS)

Who Should Register
-------------------
Any sole proprietor doing business in the Philippines under a name other than
their true name must register that business name with DTI. Corporations and
partnerships register with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
instead; cooperatives register with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA).

Territorial Scopes and Fees
---------------------------
Business names are registered for a territorial scope. The registration fee
depends on the scope selected:
- Barangay:    PHP 200
- City/Municipality: PHP 500
- Regional:    PHP 1,000
- National:    PHP 2,000
A Documentary Stamp Tax of PHP 30 is added to every application.

Validity
--------
A DTI Business Name Certificate is valid for five (5) years from the date of
registration and may be renewed within ninety (90) days before and up to
ninety (90) days after the expiration date. Failure to renew within this
window cancels the business name.

Requirements
------------
1. The applicant must be a Filipino citizen at least 18 years old. Foreign
   nationals must comply with the Foreign Investments Act and submit a
   Certificate of Authority to Engage in Business.
2. One (1) valid government-issued ID.
3. Proposed business name (must follow DTI naming rules — no generic,
   deceptive, or confusingly similar names).

Procedure (Online via BNRS)
---------------------------
Step 1. Go to bnrs.dti.gov.ph and create a user account.
Step 2. Search the proposed business name for availability.
Step 3. Fill out the application form and choose a territorial scope.
Step 4. Pay the corresponding fee through the accepted channels
        (GCash, Maya, LandBank, Bancnet, over-the-counter, etc.).
Step 5. Download the Certificate of Business Name Registration in PDF format.

Next Steps After DTI Registration
---------------------------------
After receiving the DTI certificate, the sole proprietor should:
- Register with the Barangay (Barangay Clearance).
- Secure a Mayor's Permit / Business Permit from the LGU.
- Register with the BIR and receive the Certificate of Registration (Form 2303),
  Authority to Print receipts, and official books of accounts.
- Register the business and its employees with SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG.
