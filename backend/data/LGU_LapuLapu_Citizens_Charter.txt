Lapu-Lapu City Citizen's Charter 2026 — Business Permit Services
Source: City Government of Lapu-Lapu — Business Permit and Licensing Office

About the Citizen's Charter
---------------------------
The Citizen's Charter is a document published under RA 11032 (Ease of Doing
Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018) that
enumerates the frontline services of Lapu-Lapu City Hall and their maximum
processing times.

Services Covered
----------------
1. Issuance of New Business Permit.
2. Renewal of Business Permit.
3. Issuance of Barangay Business Clearance.
4. Amendment / Retirement of Business Permit.
5. Issuance of Community Tax Certificate (Cedula).
6. Issuance of Mayor's Clearance.

Issuance of New Business Permit
-------------------------------
Who may avail: Any person, natural or juridical, intending to operate a
business within Lapu-Lapu City.

Checklist of Requirements:
- Duly accomplished Unified Business Permit Application Form.
- DTI / SEC / CDA Certificate of Registration.
- Barangay Business Clearance (from the barangay of business location).
- Lease Contract or proof of ownership of the business location.
- Community Tax Certificate for the current year.
- Locational / Zoning Clearance from the City Planning and Development
  Office (CPDO).
- Sanitary Permit from the City Health Office.
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from the Bureau of Fire
  Protection, Lapu-Lapu City Station.
- Public Liability Insurance (for certain business types).
- Two (2) valid government-issued IDs.

Service Standard:
- Simple transactions: 3 working days.
- Complex transactions (requiring field inspection): 7 working days.

Fees Involved:
- Mayor's Permit Fee: based on the Lapu-Lapu City Revenue Code.
- Business Tax: graduated per line of business (manufacturing, retail,
  services, etc.).
- Sanitary Fee, Garbage Fee, and other regulatory fees: assessed by the
  City Treasurer's Office.

Renewal of Business Permit
--------------------------
Renewal window: January 2 to January 20 of each year.
Late renewals incur a 25% surcharge plus 2% monthly interest on the
business tax due.

Requirements:
- Previous year's Business Permit and Official Receipt of payment.
- Annual audited financial statement or sworn declaration of gross sales
  for the previous year.
- Barangay Business Clearance for the current year.
- Community Tax Certificate for the current year.
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (renewed annually).

Service Standard: 1 to 3 working days during the renewal window, faster
through the Business One Stop Shop (BOSS).

Issuance of Barangay Business Clearance
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
- Barangay Residence Certificate or Cedula.
- Contract of Lease or proof of location.
- Valid ID.
- Proof of DTI / SEC / CDA registration (for returning businesses).

Service Standard: Same-day issuance, provided no adverse report from the
Barangay Tanod or neighbors.

Feedback and Redress Mechanism
------------------------------
Feedback may be filed through the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk
(PACD) at Lapu-Lapu City Hall or via the city's official feedback email.
Under RA 11032, complaints must be acted upon within the periods prescribed
by the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA).
