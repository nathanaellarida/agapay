DTI BMBE (Barangay Micro Business Enterprise) Registration Guidelines
Source: Department of Trade and Industry — Bureau of Small and Medium Enterprise Development
Reference: Republic Act No. 9178 (Barangay Micro Business Enterprises Act of 2002)

Overview
--------
A Barangay Micro Business Enterprise (BMBE) is any business entity engaged in the
production, processing, or manufacturing of products, including agro-processing,
trading, and services, with total assets of not more than Three Million Pesos
(PHP 3,000,000), excluding the land on which the particular business entity's
office, plant, and equipment are situated.

Benefits of BMBE Registration
-----------------------------
1. Exemption from income tax on income arising from the operation of the enterprise.
2. Exemption from the coverage of the Minimum Wage Law (employees still entitled
   to social security and healthcare benefits).
3. Access to a special credit window set up by government financial institutions
   (Land Bank, DBP, SB Corporation, PCFC).
4. Technology transfer, production, and management training programs through DTI,
   DOST, TESDA, and CHED.

Who May Apply
-------------
Any person, natural or juridical — such as a sole proprietorship, partnership,
cooperative, corporation, or association — whose total assets do not exceed
PHP 3,000,000, is eligible to register as a BMBE.

Where to Register
-----------------
The Negosyo Center in the city or municipality where the business is located
processes BMBE applications. DTI issues the Certificate of Authority.

Documentary Requirements
------------------------
1. Duly accomplished BMBE Application Form (in triplicate, signed by the owner
   or authorized representative).
2. DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (for sole proprietorship),
   SEC Certificate (for corporations / partnerships), or CDA Certificate
   (for cooperatives).
3. Mayor's Permit or duly notarized sworn affidavit that the enterprise is
   barangay-based and micro in nature.
4. Sworn statement of assets owned and/or used by the applicant, supported by
   a list with acquisition cost (excluding the land on which the office, plant,
   and equipment are situated).
5. Two (2) recent 1x1 ID photos of the owner or authorized representative.

Procedure
---------
Step 1. Secure and accomplish the BMBE Form 01 at the Negosyo Center.
Step 2. Submit the form together with the documentary requirements.
Step 3. The Negosyo Center evaluates completeness within fifteen (15) working
        days from receipt of the application.
Step 4. If the application is approved, DTI issues the Certificate of Authority
        valid for two (2) years, renewable for the same period.
Step 5. The BMBE presents the Certificate of Authority to the BIR Revenue
        District Office to avail of the income tax exemption.

Fees
----
Registration and issuance of the Certificate of Authority is FREE of charge.

Renewal
-------
The Certificate of Authority is valid for two (2) years and may be renewed for
another two (2) years for as long as the enterprise continues to meet the
definition of a BMBE. Renewal must be filed at the same Negosyo Center where
the original application was processed.
