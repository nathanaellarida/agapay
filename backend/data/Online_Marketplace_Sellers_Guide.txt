Online Marketplace Seller's Guide — TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada Philippines
Source: Platform seller policies (2024–2026 editions) and DTI Memorandum
Circular No. 22-05 on online business registration.

Overview
--------
Selling online in the Philippines is now governed by both the platform you
sell on and government regulation. Since 2022, DTI has required all online
sellers operating commercially to be properly registered. Platforms verify
this during onboarding.

Step 1: Register Your Online Business
-------------------------------------
Even if you sell only through TikTok Shop or Shopee, you legally need:
1. A DTI Business Name Certificate (sole proprietor) OR SEC registration
   (if a corporation/partnership).
2. A Barangay Business Clearance — yes, even for home-based online sellers.
3. A Mayor's Permit from the LGU where your stock or fulfillment address is.
4. BIR registration (Form 1901 for sole props) and a Certificate of
   Registration (Form 2303). You will need this to issue receipts and
   invoices, which platforms now require for higher seller tiers.

Tip for low-volume sellers: Some LGUs have introduced a "Negosyo sa Bahay"
or simplified online seller permit. Check your city's BPLO before applying
for a full Mayor's Permit.

Step 2: Create a Seller Account on the Platform
-----------------------------------------------
A. TikTok Shop Philippines
   - Apply via seller.tiktokglobalshop.com.
   - Required documents:
     * DTI / SEC Certificate
     * Government-issued ID of the legal representative
     * Active Philippine bank account or e-wallet for payouts
   - Approval: usually 2–5 business days.
   - Categories restricted to brand-authorized sellers: cosmetics, health
     supplements, electronics. You will be asked for an Authorization Letter.

B. Shopee Philippines
   - Apply via banner.shopee.ph or the "Become a Seller" page.
   - Required documents:
     * DTI / SEC Certificate (for "Mall" and verified seller programs)
     * Valid ID
     * Bank account / GCash for Shopee Wallet payouts
   - Personal sellers (no DTI yet) can sell low-volume but cannot join
     Shopee Mall, Brand Box, or run paid ads beyond a basic threshold.

C. Lazada Philippines
   - Apply via lazada.com.ph/seller-center.
   - Required documents:
     * DTI / SEC Certificate
     * Valid ID
     * Bank account
     * Tax-related documents for LazMall sellers
   - LazMall is invite-only or requires brand authorization plus higher
     monthly GMV.

Step 3: Tax and Receipt Compliance
----------------------------------
- Issue official receipts (or BIR-registered invoices, post-Ease of Paying
  Taxes Act) for every sale. Platforms can be audited and may share GMV
  reports with BIR.
- File quarterly Percentage Tax (Form 2551Q) if non-VAT-registered, OR
  the 8% flat income tax option if you elected it during BIR registration.
- File annual income tax (Form 1701 or 1701A).
- Withholding tax: platforms now withhold 1% creditable withholding tax
  on payouts to non-VAT sellers and remit it to BIR. You'll see this on
  your platform statement.

Step 4: Run Your Listings Like a Brand
--------------------------------------
- High-resolution product photos (min. 1000x1000px) outperform basic phone
  shots in conversion.
- Use Filipino + English bilingual titles for broader reach.
- Star sellers respond to messages within 1 hour during business hours.
- Run platform-funded promotions (Shopee 9.9, TikTok Shop Live Fest, Lazada
  Birthday Sale) — discounts are partially subsidized by the platform.
- Use voucher stacking (platform voucher + seller voucher + free shipping)
  to maximize the cart-conversion algorithm boost.

Common Mistakes
---------------
- Operating without DTI registration. Platforms have started suspending
  unregistered shops during periodic compliance sweeps.
- Mixing personal and business bank accounts. Open a separate account or
  e-wallet for the business — your accountant will thank you.
- Ignoring product compliance. FDA-regulated goods (cosmetics, food
  supplements) require Certificates of Product Notification before listing.
- Posting copyrighted brand logos without authorization. Counterfeit-watch
  systems on TikTok Shop and Shopee are aggressive.
