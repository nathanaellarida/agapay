Barangay Business Clearance Guidelines (General — Cebu and Lapu-Lapu)
Source: Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) — Barangay
Operations Manual; Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160)

What It Is
----------
The Barangay Business Clearance is a document issued by the Punong Barangay
certifying that a business establishment is located within the territorial
jurisdiction of the barangay and that it is in good standing with local
ordinances. It is a prerequisite to securing the Mayor's Permit.

Who Must Secure It
------------------
All persons or entities operating a business within a barangay, regardless
of size — including sari-sari stores, online sellers with a physical stock
point, and home-based services.

Requirements
------------
1. Duly accomplished Barangay Clearance application form (available at the
   barangay hall).
2. Valid government-issued ID of the applicant.
3. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) for the current year.
4. DTI / SEC / CDA Certificate of Registration.
5. Proof of business address — Contract of Lease, Deed of Sale, Transfer
   Certificate of Title, or Tax Declaration.
6. Sketch or location map of the business establishment.
7. Previous year's Barangay Clearance (for renewals).
8. Payment of the Barangay Clearance fee (set by Barangay Ordinance,
   typically ranging from PHP 200 to PHP 1,000 depending on the nature and
   scale of the business).

Procedure
---------
Step 1. Secure and accomplish the Barangay Clearance application form at
        the barangay hall where the business will operate.
Step 2. Submit the form with the required documents to the Barangay
        Secretary.
Step 3. The Barangay Tanod / Inspector may conduct an ocular inspection,
        especially for new applications.
Step 4. Pay the assessed Barangay Clearance fee at the Barangay Treasurer.
Step 5. The Punong Barangay signs the clearance. Issuance is usually
        same-day, provided there is no adverse finding.

Validity
--------
The Barangay Business Clearance is valid for one (1) calendar year and must
be renewed on or before January 20 of each year, concurrent with the
Mayor's Permit renewal period.

Common Reasons for Disapproval
------------------------------
- The proposed location violates a barangay or city zoning ordinance.
- The business has unpaid barangay fees from a previous year.
- Adverse reports from neighbors (noise, sanitation, etc.).
- Falsified supporting documents.
